# GAME_DESIGN.md — Zombie Survival Game Design Document

## 1. Game Mechanics & Core Loop

Players explore 7 post-apocalyptic maps, fight scaling zombie waves, gather loot drops, earn XP and coins, level up, unlock skill tree nodes, upgrade weapons, and fight major boss encounters at Wave 10, 20, and 30+.

## 2. Zombie Archetypes & Bosses

1. **Normal Walker**: Balanced HP and speed.
2. **Runner**: High speed, low HP, fast chaser.
3. **Crawler**: Low profile, high damage ankle biter.
4. **Tank**: Massive HP brute, heavy damage.
5. **SWAT Armored**: Ballistic armor plate absorption.
6. **Spitter**: Ranged acid projectile attacker.
7. **Exploder**: Kamikaze death explosion.
8. **Screamer**: Horde summoner.
9. **Shadow Stalker**: Stealth cloaked hunter.
10. **The Butcher (Boss)**: Ground slam and cleaver hook.
11. **The Mutant (Boss)**: Acid rain and toxic pools.
12. **The Overlord (Boss)**: Telekinetic blast and rage shield.
